Platinum Roadmap to 100%

Follow this recommended chronological order to earn the Platinum trophy efficiently while avoiding all missable pitfalls.

Stage 1: Main Story & Missable Story Trophies

Complete all campaign chapters (Chapters 1-4, Gone Fishing, The Cartel Heist, etc.). Keep a sharp eye on missable story triggers such as 'You Gotta Know When to Get Off'. Ensure all chapter-specific requirements are met before advancing past points of no return.

Stage 2: Side Activities, Strangers & Freaks

Meet and complete all 8 stranger strands across Leonida (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, etc.), win 20 races, beat Cal at fishing, complete arcade games, and conquer the 42 Shooting Range challenges for gold.

Stage 3: Collectibles & World Exploration

Uncover all 52 areas of Leonida, complete 250 Stunt Jumps, finish 3 Collectible Card sets, meditate at all 18 spots, WAiNK at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles, purchase 8 garage properties, and register 10 vehicles in Scooter Bros.

Stage 4: Combat Grinds & Criminal Ventures

Rob 5 bank vaults, raid illegal gang operations for all 9 gangs, spend $100,000 across shops, launder $250,000 in marked bills, score 5 kills in a single Combat Focus, and survive high-wanted level escapes.

Stage 5: Platinum Legend

Clean up any remaining miscellaneous trophies and attain 100% total game completion to unlock 'Vice Got a Price!' Platinum.
